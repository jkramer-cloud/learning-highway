DRIVING TIPS

• Read the math problem at the top of the road.
• Three, four, or five answer gates appear depending on difficulty.
• Steer with ← → or A D.
• Avoid cones, traffic, and roadblocks between gates.
• Wrong answers trigger a slowdown, spinout, or damage depending on difficulty.
• Press Space to pause or continue.
• Damaged vehicles need repairs before their next race (half their purchase price; starter free).
• Buy two-tone wraps, wheel kits, and exhaust effects using game coins.
• Progress saves automatically in this browser.
